## What we do here

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Mentor by building" icon="heart">
		Codrlabs Open brings the standards of Codrlabs' production work — what
		users expect, the constraints products run under, the bar reviews hold
		them to — into projects built in the open. Every pull request gets read
		line by line.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Build in the open" icon="open-book">
		Source, architecture decisions and the reasoning behind them are public,
		including the parts that turned out wrong. The reasoning is the teaching
		material.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Held to production standards" icon="puzzle">
		Work is mentored against the conditions Codrlabs' own products face, so
		the habits people build here are the ones production work demands.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Keep it free to run" icon="rocket">
		These are unpaid projects, so they are designed to cost almost nothing to
		operate — otherwise they quietly die when the enthusiasm does.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
