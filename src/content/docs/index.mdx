## What we do here

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Mentor by building" icon="heart">
		People learn on software that has real users, real constraints and real
		review — not on exercises. Contributors ship to a live codebase and get
		their pull requests read line by line.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Build in the open" icon="open-book">
		Source, architecture decisions and the reasoning behind them are public,
		including the parts that turned out wrong. The reasoning is the teaching
		material.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Solve a real problem" icon="puzzle">
		Every project here exists because someone hit the problem and found no
		good open alternative. Nothing is built purely as a teaching exercise.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Keep it free to run" icon="rocket">
		These are unpaid projects, so they are designed to cost almost nothing to
		operate — otherwise they quietly die when the enthusiasm does.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Getting involved

Start with [Contributing](/start/contributing/). The short version: pick an issue
in one of the repositories under
[github.com/codrlabs](https://github.com/codrlabs), open a pull request, and
expect a review that explains itself.

Questions go to [open@codrlabs.com](mailto:open@codrlabs.com).
